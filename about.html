Marty Plaskon is a 22-year-old emerging Canadian artist who began releasing music under the name “parallel” in 2020. While the early days of “parallel” were based in just production, Marty has recently elevated the project to new heights.

This success has seen his success climb, with his album, "frail", reaching 100k streams and with opening for Beach Vacation. (A Blue Skies Turn Black Production) He also toured the world with link3 in August 2025. While “parallel” has undergone a major evolution in the past year, there are many strings that tie the 5-year-long project together. Marty has been deeply influenced by the melancholic themes and lush, ambient textures of King Krule, Mac DeMarco, and Dean Blunt. Inspired by these artists’ deep involvement in their music, Marty has written, recorded, mixed, and mastered all of his music from his bedroom. Through “parallel,” Marty has always explored introspective and emotionally raw themes through reverb-soaked indie, folk, and lo-fi rock.

Marty’s passion for vulnerability stems from his desire to make a positive impact on mental health awareness and to foster meaningful connections through music.

parallel is currently releasing music through 'nothing special.', a label founded by porch kiss.
